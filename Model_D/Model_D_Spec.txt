MODEL D: STRUCTURE AND PRIORS (working specification, 2026-10-30)

Status labels: DECIDED = agreed. PROPOSED = drafted, not yet agreed.
Developed under Betancourt's principled Bayesian workflow. This document covers
Step 4 (model development). No Model D fit has been run on real or simulated data.


1. DATA

12937 trials, 42 sessions, one monkey (Sirius).
Response y = 1 for "high", 0 for "low".
Indices: s = session, j = hand (left, right), c = condition (uni, bi).

  x_stim   (stim_amp - 28) / 14.63
  x_bi     1 on bimanual trials, 0 on unimanual trials
  d_s      the session's distractor amplitude / 14.63, not centered
  x_dist   d_s * x_bi

Each session uses one distractor amplitude, between 6 and 32 um.


2. LIKELIHOOD (DECIDED)

  y_t ~ Bernoulli(psi_t)

  psi_t = gam_h[j,c] + (1 - gam_h[j,c] - gam_l[j,c]) * invlogit(eta_t)

  eta_t = b0[s,j] + b1[s,j] * x_stim + delta[s,j] * x_bi

The slope b1 is shared by the two conditions.


3. SESSION SHIFT, OPTION A (DECIDED)

The intended form is b0 + b1*x_stim + b2*x_dist + b3*x_bi, with b0, b1, b2 and b3
fit per session. Within a session x_dist = d_s * x_bi, so the data inform only the
sum delta = b3 + b2*d_s. Integrating out that split gives the same model:

  delta[s,j] ~ Normal( mu3[j] + mu2[j]*d_s , sqrt( sig3[j]^2 + sig2[j]^2 * d_s^2 ) )

Coded non-centered:

  delta[s,j] = mu3[j] + mu2[j]*d_s + sqrt( sig3[j]^2 + sig2[j]^2 * d_s^2 ) * z[s,j]
  z[s,j] ~ Normal(0, 1)

Session-level b2 and b3 are not reported, because their split would come from the
prior. Only delta is meaningful per session.


4. PRIORS

All priors are set separately per hand. Lapses are also separate per condition.

Lapse rates: 8 parameters, no session level, as in Model A
  4*gam_h[j,c], 4*gam_l[j,c] ~ LogitNormal(-2.5, 1.5)        DECIDED
    gam median 0.019, mean 0.034, 95% in [0.001, 0.152]
    Coded: gam = 0.25 * invlogit(1.5*z - 2.5), z ~ Normal(0, 1)

Intercept
  b0[s,j] ~ Normal(mu0[j], sig0[j])                          DECIDED
  mu0[j]  ~ Normal(0, 1)                                     DECIDED
  sig0[j] ~ HalfNormal(1)                                    DECIDED (may be revised)

Slope
  log b1[s,j] ~ Normal(mu1[j], sig1[j])                      DECIDED
  mu1[j]  ~ Normal(log 4, 0.5)                               DECIDED
    typical b1 median 4, 95% in [1.5, 10.7]
  sig1[j] ~ HalfNormal(0.3)                                  DECIDED

Distractor shift
  mu3[j]  ~ Normal(0, 1.5)                                   DECIDED (new parameter: wide)
  mu2[j]  ~ Normal(0, 0.75)                                  DECIDED
  sig3[j] ~ HalfNormal(1)                                    DECIDED (new parameter: wide)
  sig2[j] ~ HalfNormal(0.5)                                  DECIDED

New parameters err toward uninformative. mu2 and sig2 are set at half the width
of mu3 and sig3 so that, at d = 2 (about 29 um), the prior favors neither the
constant nor the amplitude-dependent term.


5. DERIVED QUANTITIES

Per hand, condition and session, with delta = 0 on unimanual trials:

  PSE = ( -(b0 + delta) + log( (1 - 2*gam_h) / (1 - 2*gam_l) ) ) / b1
  JND = log( (3 - 4*gam_h)(3 - 4*gam_l) / ((1 - 4*gam_h)(1 - 4*gam_l)) ) / (2*b1)

Both are on the x_stim scale. For um: PSE * 14.63 + 28, and JND * 14.63.


6. CHANGES FROM MODEL C

  Lapses       per hand x condition, pooled over sessions (C: per hand and session)
  Lapse prior  fixed LogitNormal(-2.5, 1.5) (C: hierarchical logit-normal)
  Shift        mu3 + mu2*d with its own scatter (C: b2*d only)
  Slope        lognormal per session (C: normal, allowing negative slopes)
  mu0          Normal(0, 1) (C: Normal(0, 2))
  sig0         HalfNormal(1) (C: HalfNormal(2))


7. PRIOR CHECK SCORES FOR THE CURRENT SET

Share of the ensemble beyond each draft threshold. The target is 0.1% to 5%.
These come from a quick simulation of one hand on the real session amplitudes,
not from the PyMC model, and are to be rerun properly in Step 7.

  Session PSE outside 6-50 um, unimanual        1.8%
  Session PSE outside 0-56 um, bimanual         5.2%   marginally over the limit
  Session JND outside 1-22 um                   1.2%
  Between-session PSE sd above 11 um            5.2%   marginally over the limit
  Typical shift beyond +-22 um at 6 um          1.5%
  Typical shift beyond +-22 um at 32 um         4.7%
  Session shift beyond +-22 um at 6 um          3.6%
  Session shift beyond +-33 um at 32 um         3.3%
  Overall accuracy outside 0.6-0.98             0.3%

The shift and bimanual PSE thresholds were loosened to suit the wide priors on the
new parameters. The two results marginally over the limit are accepted, since the
limits are approximate. The remaining thresholds are drafts, not yet confirmed, along with the lapse threshold of 0.15.


8. KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Amplitude and time are confounded. Distractor amplitude climbs with session order
(Spearman 0.90), and only the last 12 sessions alternate, between 24 and 32 um.
So mu2 and mu3 are associations with amplitude and could partly reflect training,
adaptation or novelty. The shift within a session is not affected, assuming the
trial types were interleaved.

mu3 is an extrapolation to zero amplitude, because the weakest distractor is 6 um.
Report the shift at 6 um alongside it. A compressive response to amplitude could
also produce a nonzero mu3 with no separate "presence" effect.

mu2 and mu3 are strongly correlated (about -0.90 from the design), so read them
jointly. sig2 and sig3 may be weakly identified.

Lapses pooled over sessions mean any real day-to-day lapse variation goes into
the session betas.

delta is on the logit scale and assumed independent of b1, though a mechanism such
as summation predicts a shift in um, which would scale with the session's slope.


9. OPEN QUESTIONS

  - Thresholds: confirm the remaining drafts, especially JND 1-22 um, lapses 0.15 and
    11 um between-session PSE scatter.
  - Was the amplitude ramp deliberate, and had the monkey met the distractor before
    these sessions? This decides whether a time term belongs in Model D.
  - Is Weber's law a goal of this model or background?
  - Target position: is a spatial response bias plausible, given the "high" target
    appears at +-14 on the screen?
  - Inferential goals (Step 1): confirm them, and any formal claim to calibrate.
  - Calibration budget: 100-200 prior-drawn fits, roughly 5-10 hours.

Planned as retrodictive checks, not parameters, in this first iteration:
bimanual slope and JND by amplitude, correlation between the hands, curve asymmetry
around the PSE (log amplitude), day-to-day lapse variation, target-side bias,
serial dependence, and within-session drift.
