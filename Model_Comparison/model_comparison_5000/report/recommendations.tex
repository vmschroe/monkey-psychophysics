\section{Recommendations}\label{sec:recs}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \textbf{Use Model~E as the main model} (D with the distractor terms fixed per hand and the criterion trend). It predicts as well as the best model found and is simpler than D. Report both scores: trial-level LOO with session-clustered standard errors, and leave-one-session-out.
\item \textbf{Report from E}: the average PSE shift and its growth with amplitude; the right hand's extra shift for any distractor; the bimanual ``high'' errors that depend on hand and amplitude; and the criterion trend. Drop the claim of reduced sensitivity, or state it as not detected.
\item \textbf{Do not attribute the criterion trend to the distractor} without new data. If more sessions are run, vary the distractor amplitude across sessions in random order, or interleave several amplitudes within a session, so that adaptation can be separated from drift.
\item \textbf{Before the slides are updated}, run a convergence check of E at its fitted values and a model-recovery study of E against B, as Vera did for B against A.
\item \textbf{Next extensions}: the frequency-probe trials and the response times (Section~\ref{sec:further}).
\end{enumerate}
