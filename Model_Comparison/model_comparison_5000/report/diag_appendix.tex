\paragraph{Checks of the leave-one-session-out scores}
The LOSO score of a session is an importance-sampling estimate, so it was checked directly in the earlier 1000-draw runs: for four held-out sessions the model was refit on the other 41 sessions and the session predicted from the refit.
The exact and estimated scores agreed to within 0.2 (Model~B, session 05-12: $-99.64$ vs $-99.59$; Model~D, 05-12: $-101.54$ vs $-101.41$; C+L, 04-10: $-61.26$ vs $-61.29$ with $k=1.03$; C+LKPS, 04-10: $-60.93$ vs $-60.76$ with $k=0.79$).
A second random seed changed the totals by less than 0.1 in those runs, and by at most \losoSeedMax\ for B, D, D+crit and E in the 5000-draw runs.
Sessions with $k>0.7$ in Table~\ref{tab:diag} are therefore not a concern at the precision used in this report.
